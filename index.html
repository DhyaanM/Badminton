Here's the story of Badminton:

  • 1860s British military officers introduce a net and competitive rules to the game in Pune, India 🏸

  • 1873: The Duke of Beaufort introduces the game at his estate, officially naming it "Badminton" 🏰

  • 1934: The International Badminton Federation is founded to create unified global rules 🌐

  • 1992: Badminton officially debuts as a full medal sport at the Olympic Games in Barcelona 🏅
